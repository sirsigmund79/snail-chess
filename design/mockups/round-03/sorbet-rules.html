How snails work

  1. Two snails, one each. Yours crawls toward their king. Theirs crawls toward yours.
  2. Every three moves, they step. After Black moves, each snail moves one square closer. The dots count down.
  3. Snails crush. Whatever is on the square they land on is gone. A crushed king loses the game.
  4. Snails are walls. Nothing can land on or pass through a snail. Knights can still jump over.
  5. Everything else is chess. Checkmate still wins.
